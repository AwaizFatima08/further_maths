# Further Mathematics Statistics: YouTube lecture series plan

A complete lecture series built on the workbook *Further Mathematics: Statistics* (`Further_Statistics_Workbook.pdf`). Each video teaches **one topic** in **20–30 minutes** and maps to named sections and worked examples of the workbook, so students can pause, work along and practise.

**Size:** 52 episodes, about 22 hours 50 minutes in total. Part A (AS) and Part B (A2) follow the workbook's order.

## 1. Audience and promise

- A-level Further Mathematics students on any board: Edexcel, IAL, AQA, OCR A, OCR MEI or Cambridge 9231.
- Promise to viewers: every Further Statistics topic, taught from first principles to exam standard, with a free workbook to practise from.
- Each episode lists its exam boards, so students can skip what their board does not examine.

## 2. Standard episode format (20–30 minutes)

| Segment | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hook and objectives | ~5% | A real problem the topic solves; 2–3 objectives on screen |
| Prerequisite recap | ~5% | One idea from an earlier episode, so each video stands alone |
| Core teaching | ~38% | Workbook notes: definitions, boxed results, derivations required by the boards |
| Worked examples | ~36% | Workbook examples solved live, with a pause point before each solution |
| Common mistakes and 'pause and try' | ~10% | The amber 'Common mistakes' box, then one Section A question |
| Summary and homework | ~6% | Key results recap, homework from the unit's practice pages, next episode teaser |

## 3. Production workflow (per episode)

1. **Script** from the workbook sections and examples listed in the episode card (no new content: the book is the single source of truth, so numbers on screen always match the answers).
2. **Slides / board:** one slide per boxed result; worked examples written live (tablet) so viewers see the method.
3. **Record** in chapters matching the run sheet; re-record a chapter rather than patching a single line.
4. **Check** every number on screen against the workbook before publishing (the workbook's values were verified with SciPy).
5. **Edit:** add YouTube chapters from the run sheet times, captions (auto-captions corrected for symbols such as μ, σ, λ, χ²), and an end screen linking the next episode.
6. **Publish** with the description template below and add to the playlists.

## 4. Playlists and release schedule

- **Playlists:** 'Further Statistics: complete course' (all episodes in order), 'AS Further Statistics', 'A2 Further Statistics', and one playlist per board (built from the 'Exam boards' line of each card).
- **Release:** two episodes a week takes about six months for the full series. Releasing the AS episodes first lets Year 12 students follow along during the school year.
- **Batch recording:** record one unit's episodes together (same slides and examples), then edit and release in order.

## 5. Titles, thumbnails and descriptions

- **Title pattern:** `<Topic> | A Level Further Maths Statistics | Ep <n>`, e.g. `The Geometric Distribution | A Level Further Maths Statistics | Ep 6`.
- **Thumbnail:** topic name in large type, the key formula, an 'AS' or 'A2' badge and the episode number; one consistent colour scheme for the whole series.
- **Description template:** one-paragraph summary · chapter timestamps · exam boards covered · workbook unit and page reference · link to the workbook · links to the previous and next episodes.
